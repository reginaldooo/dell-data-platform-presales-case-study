Capacity sizing

Two small calculators from the case study: a capacity range for the Dell storage platform, and how much of an HA VPN tunnel a race weekend of data uses.

Analysis of public information about McLaren Racing and Dell. Not affiliated, no inside knowledge, no Dell solution built or deployed. Defaults are public figures or placeholder assumptions, tagged in the repo.

An illustration of the method with placeholder assumptions. It is not a Dell sizing and the defaults are not McLaren's capacity: real protection overhead, data reduction, and growth come from Dell sizing tools and measured data (for example Live Optics).

1. Capacity: results

These are ranges, not point values. Dell's APEX billing cap is 85% of installed capacity for eligible storage, data protection, and VxRail, and it excludes servers (see ADR-0003). No prices are shown because none are public.

2. Link sizing (supporting): inputs

1,500 GB is the 1.5 TB that McLaren Racing and Dell Technologies state for the McLaren Mastercard Formula 1 Team per race weekend. Treated as a high-side assumption for what is copied.

23 Grands Prix on the 2026 calendar, per Formula 1. Dell's partnership page still states 22.
