"Published by" tells you who wrote it, with a strength tag: **Primary** is the organization's own statement or rule, **Vendor** is product or marketing material and is treated as vendor-published, not as neutral fact, **Press** is independent reporting, and **Practice** is a method reference. ## Conflicting public numbers (not used)
Public figures for McLaren's simulations per weekend (from about 1 million to about 300 million) and for data moved between track and factory (1.5 TB versus 2.3 TB) disagree across sources. This repo uses only the 1.5 TB per race weekend that McLaren and Dell state themselves [S2].

What the 1.5 TB covers. Recent statements attribute it to the Formula 1 team as a whole for one race weekend, not to every McLaren Racing series. The wording differs, though. The Dell blog says the team "generates" it, the press release says McLaren "can process" it, and McLaren's CEO says the team "pulls down" it. A 2020 Dell blog attributed 1.5 TB to a single car. This repo treats it as a team-level figure and keeps it as a medium-confidence assumption (A1).
